I have a boring administrative task and I want to gamify it by connecting it to my current hyper-fixation.

Input: **$ARGUMENTS**

Expected format is `boring task | current interest`. If I didn't give it in that form, ask me for both before continuing.

Build me a **Quest structure** that includes:

- A flavour-text framing of the task in the language/world of my hyper-fixation
- 3–5 named "objectives" (sub-steps of the real task, renamed as quest objectives)
- XP or progress markers for each objective
- A clearly defined **reward** I unlock when the quest completes — make it something genuinely satisfying that ties back to the hyper-fixation

Keep it short and punchy. The whole quest brief should fit on one screen.
