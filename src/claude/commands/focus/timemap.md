I underestimate how long things take. Help me build a realistic Time Map.

Input: **$ARGUMENTS**

Expected format: `project | my estimate | actual usual duration`. If I didn't give all three, ask me for the missing pieces.

Then:

1. Identify the **3 hidden sub-tasks** I almost certainly forget to account for in this kind of work (e.g. context-loading, decision fatigue, the "five more minutes" trap, cleanup, communication overhead).
2. Assign a realistic time cost to each.
3. Show me a corrected estimate broken down line by line, with the hidden sub-tasks called out so I can see *why* my gut estimate was wrong.
4. Suggest one "buffer ritual" I can do at the start to absorb the overrun rather than fight it.

Be specific to the project I described — no generic productivity advice.
