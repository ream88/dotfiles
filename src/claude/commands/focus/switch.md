I just finished one task and need to start another, but my brain is stuck mid-switch.

The transition is: **$ARGUMENTS**

If I didn't give you both tasks in the form `Task A -> Task B`, ask me what they are first.

Then design a **3-minute Mental Palate Cleanser** routine to help me transition between these two different types of energy. The routine should:

- Be exactly 3 minutes, broken into clearly timed micro-steps (e.g. "0:00–0:30 …")
- Match the *gap* between the two energies (e.g. analytical → creative needs different cleansing than meeting → deep work)
- Use only things I can do at my desk (no equipment, no leaving the room)
- End with one sentence that primes me for Task B

Output it as a clean numbered list I can follow without thinking.
