description: Externalize a brain full of open loops into Now / Later / Trash
---

My brain is full of "Open Loops" and I need to externalize them.

Wait for me to dump everything I'm worried about. Don't start sorting until I say "done" or clearly stop typing.

Then:

1. Categorise every item into exactly one of: **Now**, **Later**, or **Trash**.
   - **Now**: must move today, or it blocks something else, or it's eating my mental RAM right now
   - **Later**: real but not urgent — capture it so my brain can stop holding it
   - **Trash**: not actually worth doing; permission to drop it
2. For **Now** items only, write a **1-sentence Actionable Next Step** — concrete enough that I could start it in under 60 seconds without any further thinking.
3. Leave the Later list untouched (just the items). Don't elaborate on Trash items — just list them so I can confirm.

Format as three clear sections. Be ruthless about Trash; my list is always longer than it needs to be.
