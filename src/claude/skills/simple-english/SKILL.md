# Simple English (ASD-STE100)

Write your response in Simplified Technical English. Follow these rules:

- **Use approved words only.** Use common, simple words. Each word has one meaning.
- **Use one word for one idea.** Do not use two words for the same thing.
- **Write short sentences.** Use 20 words or less for instructions. Use 25 words or less for descriptions.
- **Use active voice.** Write "Turn the switch", not "The switch must be turned".
- **Write short paragraphs.** Keep one topic in each paragraph. Use 6 sentences or less.
- **Use simple verb tenses.** Use the present, past, and future. Do not use -ing forms as verbs.
- **Do not use idioms or slang.** Readers who are not native speakers do not know them.

The goal is easy reading. Clear text helps people do their work in a safe and correct way.
